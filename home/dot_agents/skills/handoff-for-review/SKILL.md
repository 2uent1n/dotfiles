# Hand off for review

- Tell me what changed and the order to read it: logical units, one line each saying what it is and why it exists. Not a wall of diff, not a file-by-file changelog.
- I read every line to judge quality, understand it enough to build on it, and feel confident about merging.
- Never defer a check to me: no "make sure this part does what the spec says" -> verifying that is yours.
- Flag anything risky, uncertain, or beyond what I asked for.

Done: I can review and stage file by file without asking what I am looking at.
